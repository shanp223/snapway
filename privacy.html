Privacy Policy — Snapway

Effective date: September 14, 2026 Contact: pixxlpickle@gmail.com

Summary

Snapway does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The game runs entirely on your device. It goes online only when you buy coins through the App Store and, if you use iCloud, to keep your coins and characters in your own iCloud account.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage data, or any other information. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

What stays on your device

Snapway saves your high scores for each difficulty, your coin balance, and the characters you have unlocked and equipped, and nothing else, on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your coin balance and the characters you have unlocked are also saved in your private iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage), so they come back if you reinstall Snapway or play on another device with the same Apple ID. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we cannot see or access it, and it is not sent to us or anyone else.

In-app purchases

Snapway offers optional in-app purchases: a coin pack and a one-time All Characters unlock. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID, payment details, or any other personal information. The app only learns that a purchase went through so it can add the coins. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the Snapway app removes your high scores from the device. Coins and unlocked characters saved in iCloud stay with your Apple ID so you can get them back. To stop saving them to iCloud, turn off iCloud for Snapway in Settings, under your name, then iCloud.

Third parties

Snapway contains no third-party software development kits, advertising networks, or analytics services. If you download Snapway from the App Store, Apple's handling of your purchase and download is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Snapway is rated 9+ on the App Store and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple, and parents can require approval for them with Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Snapway changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
