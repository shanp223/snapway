Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Your explorer walks along the road you build. Pick one of your three road pieces, rotate it, and snap it onto the glowing end of the road. Reach the portal at the end of each biome to travel to the next one. If the explorer reaches the end of the road, a short timer starts. Place another piece before it runs out.

What do the difficulties change?

Easy has short routes with nearby portals. Medium mixes up the order of your pieces and blocks the obvious shortcuts. Hard adds dead ends and a faster explorer. High scores are kept separately for each difficulty.

How do I get a higher score?

Travel farther and clear biomes quickly. The faster you connect the road to a biome's portal, the bigger your speed bonus. Collecting three crystals of the same colour also triggers a match that clears nearby obstacles and adds points.

I'm stuck and no piece fits

When the explorer is at the end of the road and none of your pieces fit, the timer stops and a Redo button replaces Undo. Redo restarts the current biome from its entrance. You can use it once per biome. If you are stuck again after using it, the run ends.

What is 1v1 vs AI?

A 90-second race against a computer opponent on the same map. The first to 100 meters wins; otherwise, whoever is farthest when time runs out wins.

How do coins and characters work?

Tap Characters on the start screen. The coin pack gives you 1,100 coins (1,000 coins plus 100 bonus coins) as an in-app purchase. Common characters cost 200 coins, rare characters 400, and epic characters 1,000. Tap a character's price, then tap again to confirm. It's unlocked and equipped right away. Characters are cosmetic and don't change how the game plays.

You can also buy All Characters once to unlock every character.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Characters and tap Restore purchases. This brings back the All Characters unlock on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Coins and characters you unlocked with coins come back through iCloud.

How do I redeem a code?

Open Characters and tap Redeem code, then enter the code in Apple's redemption sheet. You can also redeem codes in the App Store app from your account page.

I bought coins but didn't get them

Coins are added as soon as Apple confirms the payment. If a purchase needs approval (for example, Ask to Buy), the coins arrive the next time Snapway is open after it's approved. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

My high scores, coins or characters disappeared

Coins and unlocked characters are saved to your iCloud account, so they come back automatically when you reinstall Snapway or play on another device signed in to the same Apple ID. Make sure iCloud is turned on for Snapway (Settings, then your name, then iCloud), open the app, and give it a moment to sync. High scores stay on the device where you set them. If you bought All Characters, you can also tap Restore purchases.

Does Snapway need an internet connection?

No. Snapway works fully offline. Buying coins needs a connection to the App Store, and syncing with iCloud happens whenever you're online.

Accessibility

We want as many people as possible to be able to play Snapway. The app currently includes:

  • Crystals marked with a shape as well as a colour (circle, triangle, diamond), so matches don't depend on telling colours apart.
  • VoiceOver labels for menus, buttons, road pieces, and the shop. The game map itself is visual and is not yet playable with VoiceOver.
  • Fewer pop-up and screen-shake animations when Reduce Motion is on in iOS Settings.
  • A sound on/off button. Sound effects are never needed to play.
  • An Easy difficulty with shorter routes. The explorer keeps walking on every difficulty, so play is timed.

If something in Snapway is hard for you to use, email pixxlpickle@gmail.com with "Accessibility" in the subject line. We read every message and use them to decide what to improve.
