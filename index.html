Snapway

Snap road pieces together and guide a tiny explorer through an endless voxel world. Rotate, plan ahead, and find your way to the portal at the end of every biome.

  • Three difficulties, from relaxed to tricky
  • Dozens of biomes to travel through
  • 1v1 races against an AI opponent
  • 10 unlockable characters, from common to epic
  • Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collected

Snapway is free to download. An optional coin pack unlocks cosmetic characters.
